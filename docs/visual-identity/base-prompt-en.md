# 🐝 Rede Colmeia — Brandbook

## 1. Brand Essence

**Name:** Rede Colmeia
**Nature:** Decentralized community infrastructure
**Purpose:** People sustaining people, without institutional dependency

### Principles

* Mutual aid
* Decentralization
* Direct action
* Community over institutions
* Scalable and replicable

---

## 2. Positioning

* Not an NGO
* Not a traditional company
* Not charity

**It is:**

* An active support network
* A living community system
* Open social infrastructure

---

## 3. Visual Concept

* Living system
* Hive as structure
* Expanding network
* Collective movement

---

## 4. Aesthetic Direction

* Organic + technological
* Modular + fluid
* Strong + human
* Controlled imperfection (avoid rigidity)

---

## 5. Color Palette

| Color          | Hex     | Usage                  |
| -------------- | ------- | ---------------------- |
| Honey Yellow   | #F2B705 | Highlights / energy    |
| Deep Black     | #0D0D0D | Background / structure |
| Warm Off-white | #F5E9D0 | Light base             |
| Moss Green     | #3A5A40 | Community              |
| Burnt Orange   | #D96C06 | Action / urgency       |

---

## 6. Typography

### Primary

* Modern sans-serif
* Strong and legible

### Usage

* Titles: UPPERCASE
* Body: simple and direct

---

## 7. Graphic Elements

### Core

* Hexagons (cells)

### Supporting

* Connection lines
* Organic networks
* Flowing dots (swarm effect)

### Rules

* Avoid perfect symmetry
* Emphasize growth and expansion

---

## 8. Logo

### Direction

* Hexagonal structure
* Modular
* Expandable
* Abstract

### Variations

* Full logo
* Icon (reduced)
* Monochrome

### Avoid

* Mascots
* Literal bee illustrations
* Childish style

---

## 9. Layout

* Modular structure (blocks/cells)
* Pattern repetition
* Flexible grid system
* Balanced negative space

---

## 10. Tone of Voice

* Direct
* Human
* No bureaucracy
* No institutional language

---

## 11. Language

### Structure

* Short sentences
* Maximum clarity

### Examples

* No one stands alone
* From people to people
* No waiting
* This is community
* We make it happen

---

## 12. Key Messages

* Community sustains community
* The network is the strength
* Collective action works
* Everyone contributes

---

## 13. Emotional Impact

* Unity
* Safety
* Movement
* Trust
* Collective strength

---

## 14. Applications

### Digital

* Modular interfaces
* Network-based visuals
* High contrast

### Social

* Bold, short messaging
* Repeatable visual system

### Physical

* Stickers
* Posters
* Easily replicable identity

---

## 15. Art Direction

* Avoid excessive perfection
* Blend structure with subtle chaos
* Convey a living system

---

## 16. Restrictions

* NGO-like aesthetics
* Cliché icons (hands, hearts)
* Institutional communication style
* Generic corporate visuals

---

## 17. Prompt Keywords

* decentralized network
* mutual aid
* human infrastructure
* hive system
* modular structure
* organic connections
* collective movement
* grassroots
* community powered
* anti-fragile system
